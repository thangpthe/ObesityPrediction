### Target and BMI investigation

The numeric target values are observed data values only. Their semantic mapping to 'obese'/'not obese' has not been verified from a data dictionary or primary source.

- Label 0: n=8,849, BMI range 12.87–36.71, mean=23.439; BMI < 30: 8,582, BMI ≥ 30: 267.
- Label 1: n=8,849, BMI range 16.51–55.15, mean=36.734; BMI < 30: 408, BMI ≥ 30: 8,441.

- Records that disagree with a simple BMI ≥ 30 vs label 1 rule: **675**.
- This disagreement means the current binary target is not a clean deterministic BMI ≥ 30 label. It does not by itself prove or disprove other forms of leakage.

## Data quality decisions

1. Do not overwrite or resample source CSV files under `data/raw/` during auditing.
2. Treat `obesity_prediction_dataset_final.csv` as a processed/balanced candidate, not as raw data.
3. Recover the pre-integration dataset and the pre-balancing dataset before training a model intended to estimate performance on a natural population.
4. Recover the original label mapping and the rule used to derive `Obesity`.
5. Verify units and BMI generation. Investigate records with substantial BMI formula discrepancies instead of silently recalculating them.
6. Preserve a natural-distribution holdout set. Any resampling must happen only inside training folds.
